Our Communities

Our YRC community brings together dedicated student volunteers committed to the values of Health, Service, and Friendship. Through blood donation drives, health awareness, education, social service, and humanitarian initiatives, we work together to serve society and create a healthier, more compassionate community.

YRC Volunteers

YRC volunteers are the heart of our initiatives, actively contributing to community service, health awareness, humanitarian activities, and social welfare. Through teamwork, compassion, and dedicated service, our volunteers strive to create a positive and lasting impact on society.

Designing

The Designing Team creates visually engaging posters, banners, flyers, and other creative materials for YRC events and initiatives. They ensure that every design effectively communicates the message and maintains the identity of Youth Red Cross.

Social Media

The Social Media Team manages YRC’s digital presence across platforms such as Instagram, LinkedIn, and Twitter. They create and publish engaging content, share event updates, and help spread awareness about YRC’s activities and initiatives.

Technical

The Technical Team handles the digital and technical aspects of YRC activities. They create registration forms for events, manage event data, maintain digital systems, and keep the YRC website updated and functional.

Content

The Content Team documents YRC’s activities by preparing detailed event reports for submission to the Indian Red Cross Society. They also write captions and other content that effectively communicate YRC’s events, achievements, and social initiatives.

Our Initiatives

Project Education

Our initiative focuses on providing educational support to students of NGOs up to Class 10. Through regular teaching sessions, we help students strengthen their academic knowledge, build confidence, and develop a positive attitude towards learning.

Project Annapurna

Project Annapurna aims to provide nutritious and affordable meals to people in need. Every Sunday, volunteers distribute food at just ₹5, promoting the values of compassion, dignity, and community service.

Project Muskan

Project Muskan focuses on engaging and educating children through fun and interactive activities. Every Sunday, volunteers visit Bal Bhawan near MOF to teach students and conduct activities such as drawing, mental maths, dancing, and games.
